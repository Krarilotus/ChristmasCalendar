# A Crusader's Christmas

A Christmas calendar of single-player scenario maps for Stronghold Crusader by Krarilotus. This version has 21 doors: one opens each day from 1 to 21 December 2026, and opened doors stay open. Each door is a map with its own story, an overview picture and hints.

The difficulty starts around the end of the game's own skirmish trail and rises day by day. Doors 1, 6, 8 and 15 tell a continuous story; these story maps need more tactical thinking, have more challenges and come with more hints. The maps are balanced for the settings this calendar suggests, in the normal (not Extreme) Stronghold Crusader, without glitches or exploits.

## How to play
1. Activate **A Crusader's Christmas** in the launcher. In the list of active content, keep this order from the top: **A Crusader's Christmas**, then **Conquering Christmas**, then **Vanilla Retraced** and any other plugin that changes the AI, the start goods or the textures. Then the calendar's settings and the snow textures are used.
2. Start Stronghold Crusader (not Extreme) and choose **Custom Scenarios → Play a Map**. The map of each door appears in that list from its day on (Door01 on 1 December, Door02 on 2 December, and so on up to Door21 on 21 December).
3. Read the story of the door before you start: in the launcher, open **Door pages** in the options of this plugin. Each opened door shows its story, an overview of the map and hints. The hints stay folded, so only open them when you are stuck.

Restarting a map from the in-game menu does not work for these scenario maps. Save the game right after it starts and load that save to try again, or start the map again from Custom Scenarios → Play a Map. The random banners you may see on some maps can be ignored.

If a map is too hard even with the hints, skip it and play the next day's map. You can also ask friends or look for a playthrough.

## Map language
The texts inside the maps (for example the mission briefing in the game) exist in English and German. Choose them with the option **Map language** of this plugin. The German maps are called Türchen01, Türchen02 and so on. The door pages in the launcher follow the launcher language.

## What this plugin uses
- **Snow textures** from the plugin Conquering Christmas. They change the look of **every** map while the calendar is active, not only the calendar maps. When two plugins replace the same texture, the one loaded last wins. Keep Conquering Christmas above Vanilla Retraced so that the snow terrain is shown.
- **Settings** from the original calendar: the UCP2-Legacy changes it was balanced with, its start gold and goods, and its AI personalities for all 16 lords. They are suggestions: they apply while the calendar sits above other plugins that suggest something else, and you can still change them.
- The **files** module lists the calendar maps and opens one per day.

## Stronghold Crusader Extreme
The maps are made for Stronghold Crusader and aren't listed in Extreme. While the calendar is active, its settings and the snow textures also apply when you start Extreme, so deactivate it there if you don't want them.

## Coming from the old calendar
The UCP2 version of the calendar copied its maps into the game's maps folder. Remove the old Door01.map … Door21.map and Türchen01.map … Türchen21.map files from that folder. Otherwise the old copies are listed from the first day on, all at once, and may be played instead of the maps of this plugin.
