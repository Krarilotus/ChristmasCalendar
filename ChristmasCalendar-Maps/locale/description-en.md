# A Crusader's Christmas: Maps

The 21 scenario maps of the Christmas calendar by Krarilotus for Stronghold Crusader Extreme. Each map appears under **Custom Scenarios → Load a Map** from its day on (Door01 on 1 December, up to Door21 on 21 December).

Activate **A Crusader's Christmas** to play the maps as the calendar, with its trail, door pages and settings. This plugin alone only lists the maps.
