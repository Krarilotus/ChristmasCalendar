# A Crusader's Christmas: AI

The AI lords of the Christmas calendar by Krarilotus: all 16 lords with their own AIC, lord strength and start troops, tuned for AIC Tactics. This plugin selects the Hard AI in AI Swapper for every lord, together with the calendar's AI settings of UCP2 Legacy and AIC Tactics.

The AI also exists in Normal and Story strength. The difficulty presets of **A Crusader's Christmas** choose them.

Alone, this plugin gives skirmish and multiplayer games the calendar's AI lords.
