# A Crusader's Christmas

A Christmas calendar of single-player scenario maps for Stronghold Crusader Extreme by Krarilotus. From 1 December, a door opens on its day once every door before it is won or skipped with a chicken. Each door is a map with its own story, an overview picture and hints.

The difficulty starts around the end of the game's own trail and rises day by day. Doors 1, 6, 13, 20, 24 and 25 tell a continuous story; these maps need more tactical thinking and come with more hints.

## How to play
1. Choose **A Crusader's Christmas** in the launcher and pick a difficulty: Story, Normal (default) or Hard. The maps, the AI, the balance and the settings plugins are activated with it.
2. Start Stronghold Crusader Extreme and open the Extreme trail. The calendar replaces it: each door is a mission there from its day on. Each difficulty keeps its own progress.
3. Read the story of a door before you start: click its number under **Doors**. Open a hint only when you are stuck.

If a map is too hard even with the hints, skip it with a chicken, or switch to Story.

## What it uses
- **Conquering Christmas** by Monsterfish for the snow. It changes the look of every map while the calendar is active.
- **A Crusader's Christmas: Balance**: the vanilla game with bug fixes and a few small changes. Its description lists them.
- **A Crusader's Christmas: AI**: the calendar's lords for each difficulty.
- **A Crusader's Christmas: Settings**: the trail, the start goods, the fixes and the interface settings of the calendar.
