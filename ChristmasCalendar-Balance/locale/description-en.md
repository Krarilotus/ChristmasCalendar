# A Crusader's Christmas: Balance

The game balance of the Christmas calendar by Krarilotus: the vanilla game with the bug fixes of UCP2 Legacy and a few small changes.

- Blacksmith costs 100 gold instead of 200.
- Knights have 25000 health instead of 20000, as much as swordsmen.
- Arabian swordsmen die after 7 crossbow hits instead of 3.
- Spearmen die after 5 arrow hits instead of 3 and survive one crossbow hit; they run instead of walking.
- Laddermen die after 12 arrow hits instead of 5 and after 5 crossbow or sling hits instead of 2; they cost 20 gold instead of 4.
- The trading post is free.
- Arabian swordsmen tear down walls about twice as fast, and the healer heals the wounded.

The values come from Rebalancer (`resources/balance/calendar.json`), Running Units and UCP2 Legacy. Alone, this plugin gives skirmish and multiplayer games the calendar's balance.
